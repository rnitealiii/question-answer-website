Pak Navy Civilian Apprenticeship — Test Preparation

Practice tests in the same pattern as the Pakistan Navy initial test — matric level, MCQ-based, timed.

How the real test works: The Navy initial test is taken on a computer (E-Testing) at the Navy Selection Centre. It has three parts — Verbal Intelligence, Non-Verbal Intelligence and an Academic Test. Each part is timed, there is usually no negative marking, and you need about 50% marks to pass. Practice each part below, exactly like the real thing.

1. Verbal Intelligence Test

Number & letter series, coding, analogies, synonyms & antonyms, odd one out and simple reasoning.

75 MCQs45 MinutesPassing: 50%

2. Non-Verbal Intelligence Test

Shapes, patterns, mirror & water images and figure series — solved by looking, not by language.

60 MCQs45 MinutesPassing: 50%

3. Academic Test

Matric-level English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and General Knowledge / Pakistan Studies.

75 MCQs45 MinutesPassing: 50%

Tips for test day

  • Do not spend too long on one question — skip it and come back if time is left.
  • There is usually no negative marking, so attempt every question, even by guessing.
  • Calculators are not allowed — practise mental maths for percentages and division.
  • In non-verbal questions, look for rotation, movement of dots and repeating patterns.
