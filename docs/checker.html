What does the checker verify? (strict rules)
  • Speaker labels end with a colon (Speaker 1:), single space after it, no Tab key.
  • Timestamps in [HH:MM:SS] format, in chronological order.
  • Only [inaudible 00:00:00] and [unintelligible 00:00:00] markings — nothing invented.
  • Sound notes in [brackets], lower case, max two words.
  • No exclamation marks, no [sic], no (parentheses).
  • Clean verbatim: no slang (gonna → going to), yeah → yes, OK → Okay, fillers flagged.
  • Single-digit numbers spelled out (five not 5), with money/percent/measure exceptions.
  • Acronyms without periods (USA, PhD), commas/periods inside quotes, AM/PM capitalized.
  • Every sentence capitalized and properly punctuated.
  • Hindi: English words in Devanagari script, numbers spelled out in Hindi.

Note: this checks formatting & style against the guidelines. It cannot verify that the words match the audio.
